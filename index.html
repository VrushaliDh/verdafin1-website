Climate financing made easier

Verdafin is an NBFC designed for climate financing, which powers green companies and projects often overlooked by traditional banks. We fund innovative projects working towards a green, sustainable future, in areas such as renewable energy, energy efficiency and low-carbon technologies. We’re here to help India achieve its bold climate goals, combat climate change and build a sustainable future for the generations to come.
